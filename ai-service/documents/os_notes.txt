A good budgeting guideline is to track spending by category such as food,
travel, shopping, bills, health, and entertainment.

A person can identify overspending by comparing the amount spent across
different categories and time periods.

Essential expenses generally include rent, bills, healthcare, groceries,
and necessary transportation.

Non-essential expenses can include shopping, entertainment, dining out,
and other discretionary purchases.

A useful budgeting practice is to review expenses regularly and set
reasonable spending limits for discretionary categories.

When analyzing personal expenses, consider both total spending and
individual transactions.
